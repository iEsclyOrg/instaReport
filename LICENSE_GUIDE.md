## License Activation

1. Purchase a license from the [official pricing page](https://iescly.duckdns.org/pricing).
2. Launch InstaReport.
3. Enter your activation key.
4. The app validates the key online and binds it to your device hardware ID.

---

## Device Binding

Licenses are validated online against the licensing API and bound to your **device hardware ID**. A license key is intended for use on a single device.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use one license on two computers?

No. Licenses are hardware-bound to a single device.

### Is there a free version?

Licensing is commercial. Check the [pricing page](https://iescly.duckdns.org/pricing) for the current plans and any trial options.

### What happens when my license expires?

The software will stop accepting new activations. Renew or purchase a new license via the pricing page.

### Can I get a refund?

Refund eligibility follows the terms published on the pricing page. Contact support via the official Telegram channel to request one.
