## Overview

InstaReport is **commercial proprietary software** distributed under a paid license. Licenses are validated online and bound to your device hardware ID.

> For current plans, availability and the latest pricing, visit the **official pricing page**: <https://iescly.duckdns.org/pricing>

---

## Licensing Model

- **Paid license** grants the right to use the software for its duration.
- Licenses are **hardware-bound** — one key per device.
- Activation is performed **online** via the licensing API.
- Expired licenses stop accepting new activations.

---

## What a License Includes

- Access to supported features for the duration of the license.
- Telegram bot premium commands.
- Dedicated support from the official team.
- Free updates during the license term.
