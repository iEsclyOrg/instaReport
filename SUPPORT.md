# 🛟 Support

**InstaReport** offers dedicated support for its users. This guide explains how to get help quickly and effectively.

---

## Fastest Way to Get Help

The official support channel is the **Telegram bot / channel**: <https://t.me/iescly>

This is the fastest and most reliable way to reach the team. Support is available for license issues, installation problems, account questions and general assistance.

---

## Before Asking for Help

To help us resolve your issue faster, please have ready:

1. **Your platform** — Windows, Linux, Android (Termux) or Telegram bot.
2. **Your version** — check the current version in the app or on the [releases page](https://github.com/iEsclyOrg/instaReport/releases).
3. **A clear description** of the problem and what you expected to happen.
4. **Any error messages** shown on screen.

## What Support Covers

- License activation and renewal issues
- Installation and setup guidance
- Telegram bot usage questions
- Bug reports and crash reports
- Feature requests (forwarded to the roadmap)

---

## Refund Requests

Refund eligibility follows the terms published on the [official pricing page](https://iescly.duckdns.org/pricing). To request a refund, contact support via the official Telegram channel and provide your purchase details.

---

## Important

- Support is provided through **official channels only**. We cannot assist with purchases or downloads from third-party sellers.
- Never share your license key or personal credentials in public chats.
- Beware of scammers impersonating the team — the only official Telegram is <https://t.me/iescly>.
